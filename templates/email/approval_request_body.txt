The mail an employee sends from the HRMS app when a policy stops their request.
It goes to their reporting manager, with the employee and HR in copy.

Edit the wording freely; keep the {{ ... }} names as they are:
  manager_name     the reporting manager, or "HR Team" when there is none
  reason           what the employee typed, exactly as written
  request          e.g. "Earned Leave (EL) from 04 Nov 2026 to 04 Nov 2026"
  action           e.g. "apply for Earned Leave (EL) from 04 Nov 2026 to 04 Nov 2026"
  policy_note      why HRMS stopped it; may be empty
  employee_name, employee_code, designation
{% endcomment %}Dear Sir,

{{ reason }}

I request your approval to {{ action }} in HRMS.{% if policy_note %} HRMS did not allow it because: {{ policy_note }}{% endif %}

I kindly request you to approve it and forward it to the competent authority.

Regards,
{{ employee_name }}
{% if designation %}{{ designation }}
{% endif %}{{ employee_code }}

---
This mail was sent from HRMS on behalf of {{ employee_name }} ({{ employee_code }}). Reply to this mail to respond to the employee directly.
